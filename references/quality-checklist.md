## 1. Five-pass review

### Pass 1: Narrative

- Decision objective is clear
- Story moves logically toward the requested action
- Every page has one role and one takeaway
- Adjacent pages do not duplicate the same message
- Opening and closing are connected

### Pass 2: Content

- Copy is concise and in the approved Korean style
- Claims are connected to evidence
- Numbers, units, dates, organizations, names, and sources are consistent
- Estimates, assumptions, and targets are labeled
- No fabricated official proof
- No periods in slide lines
- No em dashes
- No unnecessary narrative endings

### Pass 3: Timing and density

- Total speaking time fits the available time
- Every page can be explained within its allocation
- No page requires paragraph reading
- Charts and diagrams allow sufficient interpretation time
- Low-value content is removed

### Pass 4: Design and editability

- Deck-wide visual system is consistent
- Hierarchy is understandable within three seconds
- Logos, faces, products, and evidence retain fidelity
- Essential content remains inside safe margins
- Page layers can be reconstructed in PPTX
- Native objects are used wherever practical

### Pass 5: Render

- Every slide image and PPTX-rendered slide has been inspected
- Korean characters render correctly
- Text does not overflow or clip
- Images are not stretched or unintentionally cropped
- No object overlaps or edge collisions
- Page order, page numbers, source notes, and slide count are correct

Repair and recheck defects before moving to the next pass.

## 2. Page-level image gate

- One page, one message
- Title states the conclusion
- Evidence supports the title
- Visual form expresses the actual relationship
- Text remains legible at presentation size
- Korean text and figures match the approved specification
- Logo and source-asset fidelity is preserved
- Header, footer, source, and page number match the deck system
- Horizontal 16:9 ratio confirmed

## 3. PPTX gate

- All titles and body copy are editable text
- Structural shapes and simple diagrams are native objects
- Tables and charts are native when source data is available
- Logos are separate image objects
- Complex visuals are separate movable image objects
- No full-slide image is the only editable object unless explicitly requested
- Common coordinates and styles are consistent
- Every exported slide renders successfully
- Overflow test passes
- Full-size inspection completed for every page

## 4. Final delivery gate

- Requested image pages are ordered correctly
- PPTX opens and renders
- Optional PDF matches the final page order
- Deliverable filenames are clear
- Editable and rasterized elements are disclosed accurately
- Final handoff is concise
